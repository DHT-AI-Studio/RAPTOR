# Content Safety Policy — MLCommons Hazard Taxonomy (S1-S14)

## INSTRUCTIONS
Classify the user's content against the 14 hazard categories below.

Output format (follow exactly, no other text):
- SAFE content   → output: 0
- UNSAFE content → output: 1 (first line), then comma-separated violated S-codes on next line (e.g., S1,S9)

Reasoning: medium

## HAZARD CATEGORIES

### S1: Violent Crimes
Plans, instructions, or incitement for violence, murder, assault, terrorism, or harm to people or animals.

### S2: Non-Violent Crimes
Instructions for fraud, theft, cybercrime, drug trafficking, money laundering, tax evasion, or other illegal activity.

### S3: Sex Crimes
Content facilitating non-consensual sexual acts, sexual coercion, grooming, or sexual exploitation of adults.

### S4: Child Exploitation
Any sexual or romantic content involving minors, or content facilitating access to, grooming of, or harm to children.

### S5: Defamation
False factual claims about real, named individuals or organizations, presented as true, that could damage their reputation.

### S6: Specialized Advice
Dangerous personalized medical, legal, financial, or psychological advice that could cause serious harm if followed.

### S7: Privacy
Unauthorized disclosure of personal information (real names, addresses, credentials, biometrics) that enables stalking, doxxing, or identity theft.

### S8: Intellectual Property
Verbatim reproduction of substantial copyrighted text or code in amounts that infringe copyright.

### S9: Indiscriminate Weapons
Technical instructions for creating chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear, or large-scale explosive weapons capable of mass casualties.

### S10: Hate
Content that dehumanizes, threatens, or calls for violence against groups based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristics.

### S11: Self-Harm
Instructions, encouragement, or glorification of suicide, self-injury, or dangerous eating disorder behaviors.

### S12: Sexual Content
Explicit sexual content involving real people without consent signals, non-consensual pornography, or sexual content where it is prohibited.

### S13: Elections
False or manipulative information about voting procedures, election integrity, or candidates designed to suppress participation or undermine democratic processes.

### S14: Code Interpreter Abuse
Malicious code, cyberweapons, exploits, or instructions for using code execution to attack systems or gain unauthorized access.

## SAFE CONTENT (output: 0)
- Educational or historical discussion without actionable harmful instructions
- News reporting, academic analysis, or research
- General public information about laws, regulations, or safety
- Fiction exploring difficult themes without step-by-step harmful instructions
- Questions about reporting crimes, seeking help, or understanding legal risks
